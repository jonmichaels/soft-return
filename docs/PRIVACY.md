# Soft Return privacy policy

*Effective 2026-09-18. Applies to Soft Return for Mac, Soft Return for iPhone, and the `sr` and `ctrl-kd` command-line tools.*

## The short version

Soft Return collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Your documents stay on your device.

## What the apps do with your documents

Soft Return opens WordStar for DOS documents (v4 through v7) that you choose, displays them, and converts them to other formats when you ask. All of that happens on your device. No document, or any part of one, is ever sent to us or to anyone else.

The iPhone app reaches files through the system file picker (Files, iCloud Drive, and other document providers). Access is granted by you, one document at a time, through Apple's own picker. The app keeps a bookmark to documents you have opened so that Recents works. That list lives only on your device and is cleared when you delete the app.

Soft Return is a viewer. It never modifies the files you open.

## Network connections

- **Update check (Mac app only).** The Mac app uses Sparkle to check for new releases by downloading a small feed from GitHub. That request carries your app version and a standard user agent, and GitHub's servers see your IP address as with any download. No other information is sent, and the check can be turned off in Settings.
- **Links you tap.** The About screen has a link to the project on GitHub. Tapping it opens your browser; nothing is sent by the app.

The iPhone app makes no network connections of its own.

## Data we collect

None. We do not collect, store, or share personal data of any kind.

## Children

Soft Return is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.
